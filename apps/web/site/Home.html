What the app does

Built around the game, not the profile.

01

Find players

Two-sided matching with players who picked the same sport, level, and city as you.

02

Chat & coordinate

One-to-one chat, only with players you matched with. No group rooms, no broadcast.

03

Plan sessions

Agree on a sport, a time, and a court. Keep the plan in the chat where you already are.

04

Stay safe

Report, block, or delete your account from inside the app. Real controls, not buried.

The problem

Playing sports shouldn't depend on who replies in the group chat.

  • ×Friends are busy.
  • ×Group chats are messy.
  • ×Finding the right level can be awkward.

What you get on day one

Four tools, one focus.

Everything you need to go from "I want to play" to "see you on Saturday." Nothing you don't.

Find players by sport

Pick a sport — gym, golf, tennis, running — and see partners at your level in your city.

Message matched players

One-to-one chat with the players you matched with. Confirm a time, agree on a court, show up.

Plan the session

Lock in the basics — sport, time, court — without leaving the chat. No bouncing between apps.

Stay in control with safety tools

Report, block, and account deletion are first-class controls — wired in the app, not buried.

Safety

Built for safer sports connections.

Three controls, every account, every chat. No upgrade required.

Report users

Flag inappropriate behavior so it can be reviewed.

Block users

Control who can contact you. A blocked player can't match or message you again.

Delete your account

Request account deletion directly from the app. Permanent, with a clear confirmation step.
